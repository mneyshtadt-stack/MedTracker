Privacy Policy

SheetPulse and MedTracker · last updated 26 September 2026

SheetPulse and MedTracker are two small personal web apps built and used by one person, the owner, for their own data. They are not offered to the public, and only the owner's Google account can sign in.

What the apps access

With the owner's permission, the apps read and write the owner's own Google Sheets through the Google Sheets API: an investment portfolio sheet (SheetPulse) and a medication and fasting sugar tracker sheet (MedTracker). Google Sign-In is used only to confirm that the person signing in is the owner.

Where the data goes

  • Data travels only between the owner's browser, a private server run by the owner, and Google's Sheets API.
  • It is not sold, shared, or given to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • There are no analytics, trackers or third-party cookies.
  • The private server does not keep copies of the sheet data. The owner's browser may keep a copy of the last loaded data so the page opens quickly; signing out of MedTracker deletes it.

Google user data

The apps' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's Third-party apps & services page.
